    Adversarial Actions and NADE¶

    The framework ports TeraSim's adversarial behaviour generation (#385). There are three ways to use it:

    • An adversarial action: one named NPC or pedestrian does one adversarial thing when its trigger holds. For example, it brakes hard in front of a follower, or cuts in.
    • NADE: an environment action that picks, every tick, which agent near the ego carries out which adversity. It samples them more often than they happen naturally, and keeps the importance-sampling weight that makes the run's statistics unbiased.
    • Scheduled static adversities: a stalled vehicle, or the aftermath of a collision, from one time to another.

    The layers¶

    Layer Module What it does
    Observation adversity.observation Builds a Scene of the agents in the Lanelet2 map frame. It answers TeraSim's traci questions: the leader and follower within 40 m, the lanes beside, the next signal, a pedestrian's crosswalk and nearest road.
    Triggers adversity.triggers One pure function per kind. Each takes what the agent sees and returns an AdversarialCommand, or None. adversity_command applies the location gate first, then the kind's trigger.
    Location adversity.location AdversityLocation: ANY, INTERSECTION, HIGHWAY, ROUNDABOUT. These are read from the map by the ODD's own probes, so an adversity fires where coverage records that location.
    Commands adversity.command An acceleration, a lane change or a path, planned along the lanes into a timed path (plan_command, predict_command).
    Execution actions._adversarial_execution Drives the path: it takes the vehicle from its traffic backend, follows the path, then hands the vehicle back.
    NADE adversity.nade, adversity.avoidance Candidates, prediction, challenge, avoidability, importance sampling and the victims' response.
    Actions actions.adversarial, actions.adversarial_traffic, actions.stalled_object What a scenario registers.

    The kinds¶

    The numbers are TeraSim's defaults; each can be overridden in TriggerThresholds. The accelerations come from the Intelligent Driver Model (adversity.idm), which stands in for SUMO's car-following model.

    Kind Action TeraSim Fires when Does
    BRAKE_CHECK AdversarialBrakeAction Follower A follower is close behind, and neither is stopped. The agent is not speeding up, and the follower is not slowing. Brakes at the emergency deceleration (9 m/s²) for 2 s.
    IGNORE_LEADER IgnoreLeaderAction Leader Its leader holds it back by more than 1.5 m/s² (free flow against car following), and the speed difference is at least 2 m/s. Accelerates at the free-flow rate for 2 s.
    CUT_IN AdversarialCutInAction LaneChange A lane beside is blocked only by the vehicle behind there (closer than SUMO's secure gap). Changes into that lane in 1 s.
    LANE_CHANGE_ABORT LaneChangeAbortAction LanechangeAbort Blocked as for CUT_IN. The agent is moving, and its lanelet lasts at least 1 s more at its speed. Drifts half a lane toward that side and back in 1 s.
    HEAD_ON HeadOnAction Headon As for IGNORE_LEADER, but the agent's lanelet and its leader's start more than 120° apart. Uses IGNORE_LEADER's command.
    TRAFFIC_RULE RunTrafficRuleAction TrafficRule It is stopping for a red or yellow light rather than for its leader. Drives on through at the free-flow rate for 2 s.
    MERGE AdversarialMergeAction Merge It is beside a merging lane with traffic on it, and not already changing lanes (turned 5° or more off its lane). One lane over: speeds up as if the merging vehicle ahead were not there, when that beats giving way by more than 1.5 m/s², for 2 s. Two lanes over: changes into the lane between in 1 s, when nobody there is within 2 s of headway.
    JAYWALKING JaywalkAction Jaywalking A pedestrian is off any crosswalk, with a road within 10 m. Walks straight across at its speed (1.4 m/s if it stands) × U(0.5, 2.0), for up to 5 s.
    RUNNING_RED_LIGHT PedestrianRunRedLightAction RunningRedLight A pedestrian is waiting (at most 0.2 m/s) within 3 m of a crosswalk whose light is red. Crosses it end to end at 2.8 m/s.
    STOP_CROSSING StopCrossingAction StopCrossing A pedestrian is on a crosswalk. Stands still for N(2 s, 0.5 s), then walks a crosswalk's length on along its heading at 2.8 m/s.

    The pedestrian kinds (PEDESTRIAN_KINDS) are only ever a pedestrian's, and the other kinds only a vehicle's.

    When an action fires¶

    Each adversarial action takes the entity and a required location. Its default trigger is an AdversityFeasibleCondition of its kind at that location, which holds while the kind's trigger would fire. When the action fires, the command the condition found is planned on the map and driven (following_mode: FOLLOW by default, or POSITION, as TeraSim's moveToXY does). At the end of the path the vehicle is handed back to its traffic backend.

    A condition given instead decides when the action fires. On that tick the adversity's own trigger is still asked; when it does not hold, nothing is done and a warning is logged. To require both, give the AndCondition of the two.

    from autoware_carla_scenario import (
    AdversarialCutInAction,
    AdversityLocation,
    ElapsedTimeCondition,
)

# Cuts in on a highway whenever the gap allows it.
self.register_pre_tick(AdversarialCutInAction("npc1", AdversityLocation.HIGHWAY))
# Asked once, 3 s in: cuts in if it can then, else logs a warning.
self.register_pre_tick(
    AdversarialCutInAction(
        "npc2", AdversityLocation.ANY, ElapsedTimeCondition(3.0, label="later")
    )
)

    What is a merging lane¶

    TeraSim recognises a ramp by its SUMO edge name, which contains "Ramp". Lanelet2 has no such name, so MapFeatures.merges defines a merging lane as a lane that ends into the lane on its left. It either ends beside that lane, or shares what follows with it. This holds at the lanelet itself, or along its straightest successors within 100 m (MERGE_WINDOW_M).

    A crosswalk's light¶

    RUNNING_RED_LIGHT reads the Lanelet2 traffic-light regulatory element that the crosswalk lanelet carries. That is the pedestrian signal in an Autoware map. A crosswalk without one has no red light to run.

    NADE¶

    AdversarialTrafficAction runs TeraSim's NADE loop on every tick of the run:

    1. Candidates: the max_controlled (5) vehicles nearest the ego within control_radius (50 m), and every pedestrian there.
    2. Adversity: each candidate's adversity is the first entry of adversities whose trigger fires. That gives {normal: 1 - p, adversarial: p}.
    3. Prediction: horizon (5 s) every resolution (0.5 s). The ego is predicted normal: it holds its acceleration along its lane, or changes lane when its turn indicator says so.
    4. Challenge: the candidate's adversarial future conflicts with the ego's normal future (TeraSim's AV-centred NADE). With challenge_npcs, it is also checked against the other candidates' futures.
    5. Avoidability: if no victim can brake out of the conflict (emergency brake, with a 0.5 m buffer), p is multiplied by 3e-4 and the magnitude by 40.
    6. Importance sampling: q = clip(p × magnitude (20), 0, max_is_probability (0.05)), or fixed_is_probability when that is set. At most one adversity is sampled per tick, the nearest challenger first, and none for cooldown (5.5 s) after one.
    7. Response: the sampled adversity's NPC victims avoid it with probability q = 0.2 (p = 0.99) by braking at the emergency deceleration for 3 s, or else accept it and hold their speed for 2 s. That response carries its own weight. An ego victim is never driven.
    from autoware_carla_scenario import (
    AdversarialTrafficAction,
    AdversityKind,
    AdversityLocation,
    NadeAdversity,
)

self.register_pre_tick(
    AdversarialTrafficAction(
        [
            NadeAdversity(AdversityKind.CUT_IN, AdversityLocation.HIGHWAY, 0.002),
            NadeAdversity(AdversityKind.BRAKE_CHECK, AdversityLocation.ANY, 0.001),
        ],
        seed=7,
    )
)

    It drives the NPCs and pedestrians a scenario registers, and with drive_background (the default) the traffic backend's background vehicles in the world too (#417): the TrafficManager's background traffic (background<N>), SUMO's ambient vehicles (sumo:<id>). One that carries out an adversity, or responds to one, is taken over for as long as its path lasts -- off autopilot, or published into SUMO from CARLA like a released entity -- and handed back to the backend after. SUMO's mesoscopic vehicles have no actor to take over, and SUMO's pedestrians are not driven.

    The weight¶

    Each tick of a run multiplies the run's likelihood ratio by one factor:

    • p / q when an adversity is sampled;
    • (1 - p) / (1 - q) for each candidate whose adversity challenged a victim but was not drawn;
    • the response's own factor, when one is drawn.

    A statistic weighted by this ratio is the naturalistic one. The action's weight is the ratio. BaseScenario.importance_sampling_weight is the product of every action's weight, which is 1.0 without NADE. You can read it:

    • as the built-in metric importance_sampling_weight (aggregation last; see Metrics);
    • in the trajectory log, where each sampled adversity is an adversity record with its p, its q, the weight at that point, the victims and the response (see Trajectory Log).
    • in the run's coverage file, as outcome.weight: a run is importance sampled when one of its actions is (BaseScenario.outcome_weight; NADE is), and only such a run records a weight.

    To count which kinds were sampled, register a coverage item on AdversarialTrafficAction.sampled_kind (see Coverage). Merging a batch's coverage files (scenario-coverage) then estimates the naturalistic failure rate per scenario and environment: the mean of the weighted failures with its 95% interval, the relative half width it has converged to (TeraSim's NADE stops below 0.3), the self-normalised rate and the effective number of runs (openodd's weighted outcomes, see Coverage).

    Whether an adversity is under way¶

    AdversityActiveCondition holds while a road user is carrying out an adversity. It does not matter who started it: a named NPC's own adversarial action, or NADE sampling a background vehicle. An EntityFilter says whose adversities count, and a list of kinds says which (Conditions):

    from autoware_carla_scenario import (
    AdversityActiveCondition,
    AdversityKind,
    EntityFilter,
    StickyCondition,
)

# A cut-in by anyone within 30 m of the ego, at any time in the run.
self.register_pass_condition(
    StickyCondition(
        AdversityActiveCondition(
            EntityFilter(max_distance_m=30.0),
            [AdversityKind.CUT_IN],
            label="cut_in_near_ego",
        )
    )
)
# A brake check by one of the TrafficManager's background vehicles ahead.
AdversityActiveCondition(
    EntityFilter(names=("background*",), min_ahead_m=0.0),
    [AdversityKind.BRAKE_CHECK],
    label="background_brake_check_ahead",
)

    How long it holds. It holds from the tick the road user starts driving the adversity's path to the tick it is handed back to traffic. Under a StickyCondition it says that the adversity happened. With no filter and no kinds, it holds for any adversity anywhere.

    Victims' responses. A NADE victim's response (it avoids or accepts another road user's adversity) counts only with include_responses=True. It counts as the adversity it responds to, and its message names whose that is.

    Where the state comes from. Each adversity's execution records itself in adversity_activity when it starts and removes itself when it hands the road user back. The runner clears what is recorded before each scenario.

    It is the adversity_active card. Its filter fields are the entity_count card's.

    Stalled objects and collisions¶

    TeraSim's StalledObjectAdversity and CollisionAdversity are scheduled, not sampled. StalledObjectAction stands a vehicle still at a pose from start_time to end_time (scenario seconds; None means it is never removed). Outside that window the vehicle is out of the world.

    A vehicle cannot be spawned once a run has started, so the scenario spawns it in setup, preferably out of the world (spawn.hidden). The action is a trajectory run (the engine of RoutingAction's position mode) of a held pose, with hidden_outside_trajectory. collision_objects creates two of them for a collision's aftermath.

    from autoware_carla_scenario import Lanelet2Pose, StalledObjectAction, collision_objects

self.register_pre_tick(
    StalledObjectAction("stalled1", Lanelet2Pose(1043, 30.0), start_time=5.0)
)
for crashed in collision_objects(
    "npc3", "npc4", Lanelet2Pose(2001, 12.0), Lanelet2Pose(2001, 17.0, heading=0.4),
    start_time=0.0, end_time=60.0,
):
    self.register_pre_tick(crashed)

    Construction zones¶

    ConstructionZoneAction lays out TeraSim's ConstructionAdversity along a lane (adversity.construction): from start_s to end_s metres along it, past its lanelet's end on along the straightest successors. These are the zones:

    Zone Where Objects
    warning warning_zone_length (100 m) before the start signs on the shoulder, every warning_zone_spacing (30 m)
    taper_in taper_length_in (60 m) from the start cones, from 0.3 m inside the lane's edge to the work zone's offset
    buffer buffer_zone_length (10 m) cone, cone, barrier
    work to taper_length_out (30 m) before the end construction_type: cone, barrier, or mixed
    taper_out to the end cones, back to the edge
    termination termination_zone_length (30 m) after the end signs

    spacing (20 m) is the distance between objects, scaled by 0.7 in a taper and 0.8 in the buffer. With use_dynamic_spacing and speed_limit_mph, MUTCD's spacings apply instead. A taper can be linear or curved (an s-curve). The zone closes in from side, "right" by default; TeraSim instead picked the side from the SUMO lane's index.

    From start_time to end_time (None: never removed) the objects stand in the world as CARLA props, each added by a SpawnAction and named <label>/<zone>/<n> (docs/api.md). Unlike a vehicle, a prop may appear while a run is under way. When the zone appears, two things happen:

    • clear_traffic takes the backend's background vehicles in the zone off the road;
    • close_lane tells the backend the lane is closed. SUMO then disallows every vehicle class on it, as TeraSim's full_lane mode does. The TrafficManager cannot close a lane, and says so; a TrafficSinkAction on the zone keeps its traffic out.

    TeraSim's full_lane mode places no object. Here the objects are always placed: a closure Autoware cannot see is one it drives into. At end_time the props are removed and the lane is reopened; when the run ends, the lane is reopened and the runner takes the props away with every other.

    from autoware_carla_scenario import ConstructionSettings, ConstructionZoneAction

self.register_pre_tick(
    ConstructionZoneAction(
        1043, start_s=120.0, end_s=260.0,
        settings=ConstructionSettings(construction_type="mixed", side="right"),
        start_time=0.0,
    )
)

    Urban construction zones¶

    layout="urban" lays out TeraSim's UrbanConstructionAdversity (adversity/static/urban_construction.py) instead (#445). TeraSim builds a corridor of SUMO edges across several intersections and lays out each edge on its own. Here one ConstructionZoneAction is one approach of that corridor: start_s is where the approach begins and end_s is the intersection it ends at. For a corridor, add one action per approach. Lane closure, clearing traffic and the props are the same as above. These are the zones:

    Zone Where Objects
    warning from the start, warning_zone_length (200 m) but at most 0.3 of the approach at most three signs, every warning_zone_spacing (30 m)
    work to the merge taper (with no merge taper, to 0.9 of the approach) along the closed side, 0.5 m inside the lane's edge, every spacing (10 m); for construction_type="cone", every fifth object is a barrier
    merge_signs before the merge taper merge_signs (2) signs, 50 m apart, none before the start
    merge_taper the last merge_distance (150 m) before the end cones every half spacing (5 m), from 0.3 m inside the closed side's edge across to 0.3 m inside the other edge, shutting the lane before the intersection (TeraSim's merge_before)

    An urban zone has no taper in, buffer or termination zone, and no work_zone_offset or MUTCD spacing. taper_type="curved" shapes the merge taper. The warning zone and the merge taper include their end, as TeraSim's do, so with a whole number of steps the last cone stands at the intersection. The work zone does too when there is no merge taper; with one, it stops short of its end, where the merge taper's first cone stands.

    URBAN_CONSTRUCTION holds TeraSim's urban values. They come from the UrbanConstructionAdversity source and its Ann Arbor example (examples/scenarios/ann_arbor/urban_construction.yaml): cones every 10 m in the work zone and every 5 m in the merge taper, three warning signs 30 m apart, merge signs 50 m apart, signs 2 m out on the shoulder, a 200 m warning zone and a 150 m merge taper. In the editor, the urban_construction_zone action starts from these values.

    from autoware_carla_scenario import (
    URBAN_CONSTRUCTION,
    ConstructionSettings,
    ConstructionZoneAction,
)

# The 300 m approach to an intersection, closed from the right.
self.register_pre_tick(ConstructionZoneAction(1043, 0.0, 300.0, URBAN_CONSTRUCTION))
# Closed from the left, with a shorter merge taper and three signs before it.
self.register_pre_tick(
    ConstructionZoneAction(
        1051, 0.0, 200.0,
        ConstructionSettings(
            layout="urban", spacing=10.0, warning_zone_length=200.0,
            warning_sign_offset=2.0, side="left",
            merge_distance=80.0, merge_signs=3,
        ),
    )
)

    Coordinate conventions¶

    • Triggers and NADE: everything is in the Lanelet2 map frame: x East, y North, yaw in radians counter-clockwise from +x. Gaps run bumper to bumper along the lanes. A lane's s is measured along its centreline, and t is positive to the left.
    • Execution: planned paths are MapPose vertices in that frame. The trajectory follower places them in CARLA's left-handed world (y South, yaw in degrees clockwise) through the map's offset, as for any trajectory.
    • Signals: a light's state is keyed by its Lanelet2 traffic-light regulatory element's id, and read off CARLA by adversity.snapshot.

    Differences from TeraSim¶

    TeraSim (SUMO) Here (Lanelet2 and CARLA)
    SUMO's car-following model The Intelligent Driver Model, with TeraSim's gains as defaults
    getLeader, getLaneChangeStatePretty Scene: lane chains of the routing graph, with SUMO's secure gap for "blocked"
    convert2D along an edge The lanelet's centreline and its normal
    A ramp by its edge name "Ramp" MapFeatures.merges (above)
    A walking area controlled by a TLS link The crosswalk lanelet's own traffic light
    A person's SUMO maxSpeed pedestrian_max_speed, 2.8 m/s (the fastest jaywalker)
    StopCrossing counts steps at dt = 0.1 The wait is in seconds
    is_lane_changing: heading 5° or more off the SUMO lane's angle The same, off the Lanelet2 lanelet's direction (LanePosition.heading)
    Background vehicles are adversarial So are they, with drive_background (default): the backend's background vehicles in the world (#417)
    Construction zones: SUMO vtypes for cones, barriers and signs; full_lane only closes the lane CARLA props (static.prop.*), always placed, plus the lane closed to the backend (#402)
    UrbanConstructionAdversity: a corridor of edges across num_intersections, found by the edges' road priority, with a merge taper before each intersection One approach per ConstructionZoneAction (layout="urban"), from start_s to the intersection at end_s; a corridor is one action per approach (#445)
    Urban work zone: a barrier added beside every fifth cone The barrier replaces that cone, so the two props do not overlap
    Urban work zone up to the merge taper's start inclusive, where the taper's first cone stands too Short of it, so the two props do not overlap
    Urban spacings hard-coded: warning signs 30 m, work zone 10 m, merge taper 5 m; the merge taper always linear warning_zone_spacing, spacing and half spacing (the same with URBAN_CONSTRUCTION); taper_type="curved" shapes the merge taper
    Urban warning zone of no length: one sign at the edge's start No warning zone, no sign
    Urban signs: always on the right shoulder; merge signs named by merge_signs texts On the closed side's shoulder; merge_signs is how many, all the same prop
    Urban construction vehicles and workers (excavator, dump trucks, warning vehicles) Not placed
