What each vertex is departed on: a time on the trajectory's clock (Trajectory time) or any other condition -- one per vertex -- or, with none, on arrival. The entity waits at the vertex until it holds, checked from when it gets there (in Follow mode from braking distance already, but for the last vertex). After a vertex, it arrives at the next vertex's time if that is still to come, and goes at the card's speed otherwise. Vertex N is the Nth vertex (blank and comment lines are not counted); on the last one, the run ends when its condition holds.
